About

The Scottish Highlands have inspired painters for over two centuries — a landscape of weather, light and silence that shaped a distinct school of romantic painting.

This gallery brings together the most celebrated works depicting the Highlands, with biographies of the artists who travelled by carriage and steamer to capture them. The collection spans Jacob More and Alexander Nasmyth in the late 18th century, the Victorian masters Horatio McCulloch, Sir Edwin Landseer and Peter Graham, the early Modernist William McTaggart, and the Glasgow Boys and Scottish Colourists who returned again and again to the lochs and bens.
